# Tangelo: End-to-end Chemistry Workflows on Quantum Computers

## Overview

Senicourt, Brown, Fleury et al. (Good Chemistry Company/Dow/1QBit, 2022) present Tangelo, an open-source Python package for assembling end-to-end quantum chemistry experiments on quantum hardware, supporting open-shell systems, excited states, and problem decomposition.
