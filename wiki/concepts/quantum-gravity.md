# Quantum Gravity

Quantum gravity seeks to reconcile general relativity with quantum mechanics — the two pillars of modern physics that are incompatible at Planck-scale energies.

## Key Approaches

| Approach | Core Idea | Status |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| String theory | Particles are vibrating strings; gravity emerges from spin-2 modes | Leading candidate, no experimental confirmation |
| Loop quantum gravity | Discrete spacetime geometry; area/volume quantized | Mature, but incomplete |
| Quadratic gravity | Higher-derivative terms (R², RμνRμν) added to Einstein-Hilbert action | UV-complete in some formulations |
| Holographic principle | 3D bulk physics encoded on 2D boundary (AdS/CFT) | Mathematically rigorous derivation from string theory |
