# MagNav Navigation Accuracy Metric for Civil Aviation

## Overview

Iwata et al. (Institute of Navigation, 2025) develop a navigation accuracy metric for magnetic navigation systems adapted to civil aviation, where traditional radially-symmetric assumptions break down under real-world geomagnetic conditions.
