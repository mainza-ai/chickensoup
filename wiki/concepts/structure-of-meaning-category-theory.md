# The Structure of Meaning in Language: Parallel Narratives in Linear Algebra and Category Theory

## Overview

Bradley (SandboxAQ), Gastaldi (ETH), Terilla (CUNY) derive word embeddings from singular value decomposition and overlay categorical structure via enriched category theory, deriving formal concept lattices as the categorical counterpart — connecting NLP, category theory, and quantum information notation.
