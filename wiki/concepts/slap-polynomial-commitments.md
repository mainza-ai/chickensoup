# SLAP: Succinct Lattice-Based Polynomial Commitments

## Overview

Albrecht, Fenzi, Lapiha, Nguyen (KCL/SandboxAQ/EPFL/Royal Holloway, 2023) construct lattice-based extractable polynomial commitments from standard assumptions, covering two families: functional commitments and lattice adaptations of Bulletproofs, with non-interactive security proofs.
