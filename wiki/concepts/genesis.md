# Genesis

The Book of Genesis (chapter 6) describes the arrival of the "sons of God" who interbred with humans, producing the Nephilim — interpreted by some researchers as an ancient account of extraterrestrial contact.

## Connection to Nephilim

- [[nephilim]] — The hybrid offspring described in Genesis 6:4
- [[enoch]] — The Book of Enoch expands the Genesis narrative with detail about the Watchers
