# Crypto Dark Matter on the Torus: Oblivious PRFs from Shallow PRFs and TFHE

## Overview

Albrecht, Davidson, Deo, Gardham (KCL/SandboxAQ/Zama/University of Surrey, 2023) construct partially oblivious PRFs (POPRFs) on the torus using shallow PRFs and TFHE, enabling private computation of PRF evaluations with applications to private set intersection and oblivious function evaluation.
