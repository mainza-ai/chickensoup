# Quantum Lattice Enumeration in Limited Depth

## Overview

Bindel, Bonnetain, Tiepelt, Virdia (SandboxAQ/KASTEL/NOVA, 2023) apply quantum backtracking algorithms to lattice point enumeration with bounded depth, extending quantum speedups beyond sieving to enumeration algorithms critical for Shortest Vector Problem (SVP) cryptanalysis.
