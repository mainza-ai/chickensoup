# Physicist It Was Hiding in Plain Sight

Interview transcript: Professor Philip Mannheim on the ToE podcast with Kurt Jaungle discussing conformal gravity as a solution to dark matter and quantum gravity.

## Overview

The "missing mass" of the dark matter problem isn't missing — it's the rest of the visible universe, hiding in plain sight. Mannheim argues the graviton is neither composite nor fundamental; it doesn't exist. His position: Einstein's General Relativity was built on a flawed extrapolation from the solar system to all scales, and a fourth-order conformal-gravity theory explains galactic rotation curves without dark matter.

## Key Arguments

### Why Einstein Gravity Is Incomplete

- Newton's law (Poisson equation, second-order derivative → 1/r potential) was derived only for the solar system
- When Einstein generalized it via the Einstein equations, he imposed that same second-order equation universally — on galaxies, cosmology, etc.
- But there is no principle selecting second order: del⁴Φ = ρ gives 1/r + r; del⁶Φ = ρ gives 1/r + r + r³
- Edington (c. ~1920) noted the lack of uniqueness: you can derive Einstein equations from varying the Ricci scalar **squared** too, which introduces extra terms
- Without a fundamental principle fixing the order, dark matter and dark energy could be artifacts of the wrong extrapolation

### Conformal Gravity

- Starts from a **renormalizable** theory of gravity (Weyl tensor squared action) rather the Einstein-Hilbert action
- Fourth-order equations of motion; tractable only after computer algebra
- Outside a static spherically symmetric source: yields **two solutions** — 1/r (Newtonian) and a **linear potential** (r term)
- The average of 1/r (falling) + r (rising) is flat → naturally reproduces flat galactic rotation curves without dark matter
- Conformal symmetry requires no mass scale in the Lagrangian; mass can arise spontaneously (Goldstone mechanism) through the vacuum — calls this the "god vacuum" instead of the "god particle"

### Quantum Gravity

- Key distinction: "quantum gravity" should mean a theory of gravity whose low-energy limit is Einstein's, not quantizing Einstein gravity itself
- Electromagnetism survives quantization because Maxwell's equations have a dimensionless coupling (renormalizable); Einstein gravity does not
- If QFT had developed before Einstein, physicists would have sought a renormalizable theory of gravity first

### Cosmological Constant Problem

- Spontaneous symmetry breaking in the electroweak phase transition releases vacuum energy ~10⁶⁰× larger than observed cosmological constant
- Supersymmetry solves this only if exact — broken SUSY gives Large Λ again
- Conformal symmetry offers an alternative: no fundamental scalar field; mass is dynamical

### The Ghost Problem

- Conformal gravity has states of negative norm — "ghosts" — which make it non-unitary
- Mannheim's strategy: write the theory, solve it, and let nature decide; if it works, nature will solve the ghost problem
- The same issue plagued PT-symmetric quantum mechanics and π- Villar regularization before resolution

### Higgs Bosol: Elementary or Composite?

- If the 125 GeV Higgs is elementary (in the Lagrangian): "god particle," hierarchy problem remains
- If composite (dynamical bound state): "god vacuum" generates mass, conformal symmetry restored, hierarchy problem solved
- LHC non-discovery of superpartners is a serious challenge to the Standard Model and favors the composite-Higgs path
