# TurboTLS: TLS Connection Establishment with 1 Less Round Trip

## Overview

Aguilar-Melchor, Bailleux, Goertzen, Guinet, Joseph, Stebila (SandboxAQ/University of Waterloo, 2023) reduce TLS handshake latency by 1 RTT using UDP transport and piggybacked TCP three-way handshake, with implications for low-latency quantum-resistant network protocols.
