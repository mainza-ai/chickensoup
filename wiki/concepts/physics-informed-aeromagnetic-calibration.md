# Physics-Informed Aeromagnetic Calibration of MagNav

## Overview

Favour Nerrise (Stanford) and Andrew Sosanya, Patrick Neary (SandboxAQ, 2024) apply liquid time-constant (LTC) neural networks to calibrate magnetic navigation systems for aircraft, replacing GPS with geomagnetic field mapping.
