# IDOLpro: Guided Multi-Objective Generative AI for Drug Design

## Overview

Kadan, Ryczko, Yamazaki et al. (SandboxAQ, 2025) present a modular guided diffusion framework (IDOLpro) for multi-objective molecular generation in structure-based drug design, with controllable properties across chemical space.
