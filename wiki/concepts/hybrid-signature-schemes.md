# Hybrid Signature Schemes

## Overview

Bindel (SandboxAQ) and Hale (Naval Postgraduate School, 2023) propose tailored hybrid/composite PQC signature schemes combining Dilithium or Falcon with RSA or DSA, achieving proof composability and weak separability goals that simple concatenation cannot.
