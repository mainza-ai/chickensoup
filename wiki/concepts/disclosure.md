# UFO Disclosure

UFO disclosure refers to the process by which governments acknowledge the existence of unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) and any recovered non-human craft or biological materials.

## Key Events

- **2023** — David Grusch congressional testimony claiming the U.S. has run a secret UAP retrieval program since the 1930s
- **1963** — JFK assassination, connected by some theorists to a planned UFO disclosure announcement
- **Ongoing** — NASA UAP independent study team, AARO investigations
