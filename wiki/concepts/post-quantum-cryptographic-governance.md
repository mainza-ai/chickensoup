# Post-Quantum Cryptographic Assemblages and the Governance of the Quantum Threat

## Overview

"Post-Quantum Cryptographic Assemblages and the Governance of the Quantum Threat" (Csenkey & Bindel, August 2022) applies **assemblage theory** to analyze how the quantum cybersecurity threat is constructed, governed, and managed across state and non-state actors.

## Key Contributions

- Uses assemblage theory to map the "quantum threat" as a complex socio-technical construct
- Analyzes policy/strategy documents from 6 traditionally allied states: Australia, Canada, EU, New Zealand, UK, US
- Identifies six governance linkages: **Infrastructure**, **Standardization**, **Education**, **Partnerships**, **Economy**, **Defence**
- Shows how private/academic sectors have been primary drivers; states are now actively engaging
- Explores how traditional defence cooperation channels are strained by the complexity of quantum technology governance
