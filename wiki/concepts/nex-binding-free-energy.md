# Nonequilibrium Chimeric Switching (NEX) for Binding Free Energy

## Overview

Pitman, Sood, Rufa, Huntington (SandboxAQ, 2026) present NEX, a free energy framework using nonphysical intermediate states to stabilize binding free energy calculations across chemical space, improving robustness of alchemical drug design predictions.
