# Quantum PES via Adiabatic Density Transitions

## Overview

James Brown (Good Chemistry Company, 2023) shows that chemically-accurate potential energy surfaces can be generated from quantum computers by measuring only electron density along adiabatic transitions — bypassing quantum phase estimation and using real-space TDDFT Kohn-Sham potential inversion.
