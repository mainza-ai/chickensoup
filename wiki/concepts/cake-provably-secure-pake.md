# CAKE Requires Programming: Provable PQC Security of (O)CAKE

## Overview

Hövelmanns, Hülsing, Kudinov, Ritsch (Eindhoven/SandboxAQ, 2025) revisit the post-quantum security proof of KEM-based PAKE protocols, filling the gap between classical and quantum security models for OCAKE and related constructions.
