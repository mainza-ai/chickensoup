# Massively Parallel Quantum Chemistry: PFAS on 1M+ Cloud vCPUs

## Overview

Alan E. Rask, Lee Huntington et al. (Good Chemistry/SandboxAQ/Accenture/AWS, 2023) demonstrate cloud-scale quantum chemistry computation on over 1 million vCPUs, applying PFAS correlated-electron methods to environmental contaminants at industrial scale.
