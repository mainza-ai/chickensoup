# Towards Structure-Preserving Quantum Encodings

## Overview

"Towards Structure-Preserving Quantum Encodings" (Parzygnat, Bradley, Vlasic, Pham, December 2024, arXiv:2412.17772) proposes **category theory** as the natural mathematical framework for analyzing quantum encodings that respect structural properties of datasets and learning tasks.

## Key Contributions

- **Category-theoretic framework** for quantum encodings: organizes and isolates structure in datasets using category theory
- Applies framework to: geometric quantum machine learning, quantum metric learning, topological data analysis
- Provides a language for mathematically precise questions about encoding/circuit design for QML tasks
- Extends beyond symmetry-based encoding design (the current state of the art)
