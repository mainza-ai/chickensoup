# Optimizing Electronic Structure on a Trapped-Ion Quantum Computer

## Overview

Kawashima, Lloyd, Coons, Nam, Matsuura, Garza, Johri, Huntington, Senicourt, Maksymov, Nguyen, Kim, Alidoust, Zaribafiyan, Yamazaki (Nature Communications Physics, 2021) experimentally demonstrate problem-decomposition techniques for electronic structure simulations on trapped-ion quantum hardware.
