# Offline Recovery of Magic and Entanglement from Noisy Pauli States

## Overview

"Offline recovery of magic and entanglement from noisy Pauli product states" (Lloyd et al., arXiv:2505.04743, June 2026) investigates whether low-fidelity quantum states — typically discarded — retain useful computational resources (magic and entanglement) that can be recovered via classical purification.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates **magic and entanglement recovery** from noisy states generated by Pauli product formulas (common quantum algorithm subroutines)
- Fidelity of purified state depends on **magnitude and order** of magic/entanglement generation — informs ansatz operator ordering
- Consistent results across **simulation and IonQ Aria quantum device** experiments
- Key finding: **correlations within a state are more robust to noise than magic** — algorithms should target low-error correlated states
- Provides a framework for analyzing and optimizing quantum algorithms in a noisy regime
