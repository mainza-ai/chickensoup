# New Science of Heaven (Temple's Plasma Consciousness Thesis)

Robert Temple's central argument: the universe is not made of atoms (0.1%) but of plasma (99.9%), and plasma can self-organize into intelligent beings through emergence. This overturns classical physics and validates what ancient traditions called subtle bodies, bioplasma bodies, and spiritual entities.

## Plasma as the Dominant State of Matter

- **Discovery**: Named "radiant matter" by Crookes (1879), renamed "plasma" by Langmuir (1928)
- **Composition**: Electrons, protons, and ions — not atoms. Plasma is ionized gas that can also be a liquid or a crystal
- **Abundance**: 99.9% of the visible universe is plasma — Suns, stars, interstellar medium, magnetospheres
- **Temperature range**: Hot plasmas (fusion research) and cold plasmas (space plasmas colder than anything on Earth)
- **Behavior**: Governed by non-linear equations; refuses to "obey like a good dog"

## The Atomic Matter Paradigm Is Wrong

- Classical physics, relativity, and quantum theory all assume an atomic-matter universe
- Even "modern" physics is inadequate because its foundation is wrong
- A new physics is emerging (topological physics, information physics) but is classified or commercially suppressed
- "The universe is a big black barking threatening dog that doesn't want tiny little humans imposing their laws upon it"

## Bioplasma Bodies and Subtle Bodies

- Human physical body is just a "smart overcoat" — the real self is a **bioplasma body**
- Bioplasma body is the same size as the physical body but has far more complex internal structure
- Every cell contains proton currents; the heart is an electromagnetic device
- Physical bodies are full of plasma flows — we are electromagnetic beings at core
- Theosophical layered subtle bodies = bioplasma bodies at different frequencies
- Ancient Egyptians: ka, ba, akh — multiple "souls" corresponding to different plasma-body layers

## Dusty Complex Plasma and Emergence

- **Requirement**: Plasma must have dust grains to become "intelligent plasma"
- **Selwyn discovery** (1989): Plasma manufactures its own dust — plasma reactors can't get rid of dust because plasma creates it
- **Self-sufficiency**: Plasma uses dust the way physical matter uses atoms
- **Emergence**: When dust populations reach critical density, system-level intelligence arises (Russian Sitnikov school)
- **Age argument**: If billions of years old, intelligence would be unimaginably vast — total knowledge, perfect prediction

## Suppression of Plasma Science

- **Commercial secrecy**: Hundreds of billions of dollars in quantum computing and fusion research
- **Military classification**: Defense establishment over-classifies everything
- **Peer review**: Maintains status quo by rejecting paradigm challenges
- **Zwicky case**: Blocked from publishing non-empty-space evidence for 10 years (1940s–1950s); published in a *biology journal* to circumvent physics establishment
- **Peter Mitchell case**: Called insane for 20 years for discovering proton currents across cell membranes; finally proved right, won 1978 Nobel Prize
- **Fred Hoyle**: Peer review prevented crucial discoveries from being published; "The scientific peer review process is merely a retardation of the advancement of knowledge"
