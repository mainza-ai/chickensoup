# Hyperdeterminants Are Hard in Four Dimensions

## Overview

Anand Kumar Narayanan (SandboxAQ, 2025) proves NP-hardness of computing hyperdeterminants and deciding hyperdeterminant-zero for tensors in four or more dimensions under randomized reductions, extending the computational complexity boundary beyond matrices and permanents.
