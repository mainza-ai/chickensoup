# The Need for Being Explicit: Failed Implicit Lattice Certificates

## Overview

Bindel and McCarthy (SandboxAQ, 2022) prove impossibility of constructing efficient implicit certificates from lattice assumptions, clarifying the boundary between what can and cannot be achieved in lattice-based public-key infrastructure.
