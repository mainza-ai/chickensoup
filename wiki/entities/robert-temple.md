# Robert Temple

Author and plasma-physics researcher best known for *The Sirius Mystery* (1976) and *A New Science of Heaven* (2024), which argues that dusty complex plasma clouds in space are conscious, intelligent entities — and that this knowledge has been systematically suppressed.

## Key Work

- **A New Science of Heaven** (2024): Synthesizes plasma physics, ancient wisdom traditions, and consciousness studies into a unified thesis that the universe is 99.9% plasma, not atomic matter, and that plasma can give rise to intelligence through emergence
- **The Sirius Mystery** (1976): Argued the Dogon people's astronomical knowledge came from extraterrestrial contact; established his pattern of challenging mainstream narratives
- **2020 paper** with Chandra Wickramasinghe: First scientific treatment of Kordylewski clouds as dusty complex plasmas capable of intelligence, published in *Advances in Astrophysics*

## Kordylewski Clouds Research

Temple discovered the 2019 Hungarian confirmation of the Kordylewski clouds and contacted the lead researcher:

- Hungarian astronomers had confirmed existence but were studying only celestial mechanics
- Temple recognized the plasma-physics implications that the astronomers "didn't seem to know about"
- Initiated the Wickramasinghe collaboration to publish the plasma-intelligence paper
- Temple is listed as the discoverer of the plasma relevance of the Kordylewski clouds in the astrophysics literature

## Plasma Physics Argument

- Classical physics is wholly inadequate because it assumes an atomic-matter universe
- The universe is 99.9% plasma (Sun, interstellar medium, magnetosphere)
- Plasma behavior is governed by non-linear equations; it "won't sit and obey like a good dog"
- A new physics is already emerging in topological physics and information physics but is suppressed by commercial and military secrecy
- Gary Selwyn (IBM, 1989) proved plasma manufactures its own dust — the key to dusty complex plasma intelligence

## Suppression Thesis

Temple argues plasma science is the most suppressed field because it overturns the atomic-matter foundation of modern physics:

- Military and commercial interests classify plasma research
- Peer review maintains the status quo by rejecting paradigm-challenging work
- Zwicky blocked for 10 years; Peter Mitchell called insane for 20 years before Nobel Prize
- "The scientific peer review process is merely a retardation of the advancement of knowledge" — Fred Hoyle

## Ancient Wisdom Connection

Temple connects plasma science to perennial wisdom:

- Theosophical subtle bodies = bioplasma bodies at different frequencies
- Ancient Egyptians had multiple "souls" (ka, ba, akh) corresponding to plasma-body layers
- Gnostic Marcosians described gigantic entities between Earth and Moon; **Metatron** may be the Samaritan name for a Kordylewski cloud
- Blavatsky institutionalized this wisdom because it was "wiggling about too much"
