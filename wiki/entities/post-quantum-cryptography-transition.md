# Post-Quantum Cryptography Transition

## Overview

"Transitioning Organizations to Post-Quantum Cryptography" (Nature, Vol 605, 12 May 2022, pp. 237) is a **Nature Perspective** article that addresses the organizational and technical challenges of migrating from classical public-key infrastructure to quantum-resistant systems before large-scale quantum computers become operational.

## Key Points

- **Timeline urgency**: NIST's PQC standardization process is the primary roadmap
- **Lattice-based schemes** (Dilithium, Falcon) are the leading candidates for digital signatures
- **Migration challenges**: backward compatibility, certificate transparency, hardware security modules, supply chain
- **Governance dimension**: international standards bodies, national security implications, export controls
- Calls for coordinated, multi-stakeholder action across government, industry, and academia
