# Ginestra Bianconi

Theoretical physicist at Queen Mary University of London. Developed the **entropic action theory of gravity**, deriving modified Einstein equations from quantum relative entropy between spacetime metric and matter-induced metric.

## Background

- Professor, School of Mathematical Sciences, Queen Mary University of London
- Research focuses on statistical mechanics of gravity, network theory, and quantum information approaches to spacetime
- Extended earlier discrete-setting results to a continuum, fully Lorentz invariant theory

## Key Contributions

- **Entropic action for gravity**: Postulates that the action for gravity is the quantum relative entropy between the metric of spacetime and the metric induced by matter fields
- **G-field**: Introduces an auxiliary field acting as Lagrangian multipliers, yielding a dressed Einstein-Hilbert action with an emergent positive cosmological constant
- **Topological matter fields**: Describes bosonic matter via Dirac-Kähler formalism as direct sum of 0-form, 1-form, and 2-form
- **Second-order equations**: The modified gravity equations remain second order in the metric and G-field, avoiding higher-derivative instabilities
