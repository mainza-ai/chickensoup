# The New Science of UAP

## Overview

"The New Science of Unidentified Aerospace-Undersea Phenomena (UAP)" (Knuth et al., arXiv:2502.06794, March 2025) is a **195-page comprehensive review** by Kevin H. Knuth and 26 co-authors from multiple institutions. It is the most extensive academic treatment of UAP to date.

## Highlights (from paper)

- UAP are a long-standing global phenomenon studied by professional engineers, scientists, and astronomers
- Serious academic and private efforts now apply multi-messenger astronomy-style instrumentation to UAP study
- Review covers: sensor calibration, data analysis methods, instrumentation challenges, atmospheric effects, instrumental artifacts
- Proposes a science-led, non-classified approach to UAP data collection and analysis
- Establishes UAP as a legitimate interdisciplinary scientific subject rather than fringe conspiracy

## Significance

This is the first large-scale, multi-author academic review that treats UAP as a genuine scientific question rather than dismissing it. It aligns with [[disclosure]] trends and validates the [[physics-of-time-travel]] framing — if UAP represent advanced technology, understanding their physics is prerequisite to reverse-engineering field-manipulation capabilities.
