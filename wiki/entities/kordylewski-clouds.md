# Kordylewski Clouds

Massive, almost entirely transparent dusty plasma clouds located at the Earth-Moon L4 and L5 Lagrange points — 60 degrees ahead of and behind the Moon in its orbit. Each cloud is roughly nine times the diameter of Earth; together they span eighteen Earth diameters.

## Physical Properties

- **Location**: Earth-Moon triangular Lagrange points L4 and L5, where Earth's and Moon's gravitational pulls effectively neutralize
- **Composition**: Complex dusty plasma — space dust grains that attract charge carriers (protons, electrons) and self-organize into coherent cloud structures
- **Detectability**: Nearly invisible; emit no light of their own. First observed 1961 by Polish astronomer Kordylewski, confirmed 2019 by Hungarian astronomers
- **Transience**: Exhibit intermittent visibility depending on viewing angle and lighting conditions

## Burns' Auroral Circuit Hypothesis

Geophysicist Stefan Burns proposes Kordylewski clouds may participate in Earth's auroral circuits:

- During **full moon**, the Moon passes through Earth's magnetotail
- The Moon develops a powerful dayside-to-nightside electric field gradient (positive charge on sunlit side, negative on dark side)
- A Kordylewski cloud entering the magnetotail could act as a **plasma conductor**, completing a circuit that amplifies geomagnetic activity
- **80 years of geomagnetic data (1874–1954)** show disturbances peak slightly after full moon, consistent with L4/L5 cloud positions in the magnetotail
- During **new moon**, the Moon sits sunward of Earth, shielding it from solar wind — geomagnetic activity is lowest

## Temple's Intelligence Hypothesis

Robert Temple and Chandra Wickramasinghe's 2020 paper argues dusty complex plasmas can generate intelligence via emergence:

- **Self-organization**: Plasma manufactures its own dust (Gary Selwyn, 1989); without external dust it is self-sufficient
- **Emergence**: When dust grain populations reach critical density, system-level intelligence can arise (Sitnikov, Russian plasma school)
- **Age argument**: If billions of years old, intelligence would dwarf human capabilities — total knowledge of Earth history, predictive modeling
- **Ancient witness**: Gnostic Marcosians described gigantic conscious entities between Earth and Moon; one named **Metatron** ( Samaritan angel of the Lord ) may correspond to these clouds
- **Communication attempts**: Temple claims publishers removed a section on cloud-to-human communication attempts from his book

## Suppression History

- 1961: Kordylewski observed clouds but Polish communist government blocked his work
- 1961–1971: Zwicky blocked from publishing evidence of non-empty space for 10 years
- 2019: Hungarian astronomers confirmed existence but studied only celestial mechanics, not plasma behavior
- Temple had to initiate plasma-physics inquiry himself because the astronomers "didn't know what he was really asking"
